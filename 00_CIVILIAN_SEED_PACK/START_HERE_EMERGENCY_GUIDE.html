1. Immediate Life Support Triage

  • Severe Bleeding: Apply hard, direct pressure with a clean cloth. If arterial (spurting), apply a tourniquet 2-3 inches above the wound (never over a joint). Note the exact application time.
  • Water Purification (Rule of 1 or 2):
    • Boiling: Bring water to a rolling boil for a full 1 minute (3 minutes at high altitude).
    • Household Bleach (Emergency Triage): Add 2 drops of plain, unscented household bleach per 1 quart/liter of clear water (4 drops if cloudy). Mix and let stand for 30 minutes before drinking.
